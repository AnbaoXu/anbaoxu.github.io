Short Bio

An-Bao Xu is currently an Associate Professor with the College of Mathematics and Physics, Wenzhou University. He received the Ph.D. degree from Hunan University, Changsha, China, in 2017. From 2014 to 2016, he was an exchange Ph.D. student with the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, Auburn University, USA, and he was also a visiting student at the Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (2016.11–2017.05). He has led two research projects, respectively funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (Youth Program) and the National Scholarship for Building High Level University. His research interests center on the design and analysis of algorithms for matrix and tensor recovery (including deep learning approaches), reconstruction algorithms for compressed sensing, and solutions to constrained matrix equations, with applications in image and video processing and signal compression.
